Championship leader conversion at the current-season cutoff

How often did the driver or constructor leading at this point in the season go on to win the championship?

2026-10-11

Answer

At the cutoff (round 17 of 2026, the Singapore Grand Prix on 2026-10-11):

  • Drivers: the championship leader after round 17 became champion in 25 of 28 (89.3%) of historical seasons.
  • Constructors: the leader became champion in 26 of 28 (92.9%) of historical seasons.

Cutoff definition

“This point in the season” is the latest 2026 round with results in the local data (driver_standings_by_round), not a scheduled future race. Historical seasons are compared at the same round number; seasons that never reached that round drop out.

Data limitations

  • The cutoff is the latest round with results in the local DuckDB file (uv run build), so the numbers move as rounds are added.
  • Historical comparisons use the same round number as the cutoff and exclude seasons without that many completed rounds.
  • Tied leaders are included as co-leaders when they share first place; the season counts as converted if either becomes champion.
  • Constructors’ championship conversion counts only seasons that have constructor standings and a constructors’ champion.
