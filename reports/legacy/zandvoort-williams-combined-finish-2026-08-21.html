3 · Zandvoort history — the tightest signal

Query — Williams combined total at Zandvoort, by season
SELECT cr.season, sum(cr.position::INT) AS combined_total
FROM combined_results cr
JOIN races ra ON cr.season = ra.season AND cr.round = ra.round
WHERE ra.race_name ILIKE '%dutch%'
  AND cr.constructor_id ILIKE '%williams%'
GROUP BY cr.season
ORDER BY cr.season;

Four of five seasons land inside the shaded 28–33 band. Orange = 2025, an 18 built on Albon P5 from grid 15 — the same driver pairing racing now, so relevant but optimistic.

Why the circuit compresses outcomes

Query — grid-to-finish volatility, all teams
SELECT cr.grid, cr.position
FROM combined_results cr
JOIN races ra ON cr.season=ra.season AND cr.round=ra.round
WHERE ra.race_name ILIKE '%dutch%'
  AND cr.season >= 2021 AND cr.grid > 0;
-- median |finish - grid| = 3 places

Zandvoort 2021–25, 97 starts: median position change 3 places (mean 3.6). Only 30 of 97 (31%) moved 5 or more places. Low overtaking caps the upside and the disasters.
