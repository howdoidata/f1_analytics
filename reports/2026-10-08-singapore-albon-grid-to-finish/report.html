1 · Type of analysis and why

Method: Monte Carlo simulation. The race is simulated 500,000 times (25 runs of 20,000). Each simulated race draws every uncertain ingredient at random from real 2026 results, and the answer is read from where the outcomes pile up.

Why a simulation, not a single formula What it means here
The outcome is two different races in one When Albon finishes he averages +1.5 places; when he retires, −3.3. An average of the two describes a race that rarely happens.
His grid slot is unknown Qualifying has not run and the starting slot changes the answer, so each simulated race draws a grid slot too.
Only 16 races of evidence The simulation carries that uncertainty through to the result instead of hiding it in one number.
